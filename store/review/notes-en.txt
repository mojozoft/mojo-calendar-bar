Mojo Calendar Bar is a menu bar app (no Dock icon). After launch, click the calendar icon showing today's date in the menu bar to open the calendar.

What it adds over the built-in macOS clock/calendar:
- Thai lunar dates (waxing/waning moon) for every day, calculated on device
- Buddhist holy days (Wan Phra) marked under the day number
- Thai public holidays and Buddhist observances (Makha/Visakha/Asalha Bucha, Buddhist Lent, Songkran, Loy Krathong)
- Year systems: Gregorian, Buddhist Era, Japanese (Reiwa), Republic of China (Minguo)
- UI in 10 languages with locale-aware week start and week numbers
- Customizable menu bar icon: filled/outline/plain, 9 colors, day / day+month / day+month+year

To see the Thai features in any locale: click the gear button at the bottom-left of the calendar (or press Command-comma) to open Settings > Thai calendar tab > turn on Buddhist holy days / Thai holidays / Waxing and waning moon. The Icon tab changes the menu bar icon (style, color, date format). Click any day to see its lunar date and holiday names. The language button (e.g. "EN") switches the UI language.

No account, no network access, no data collection. "Open at login" uses SMAppService and is off until the user turns it on.
