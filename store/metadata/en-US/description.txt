A small, fast calendar that lives in your menu bar — today's date is always one glance away.

THAI CALENDAR, BUILT IN
• Waxing and waning moon for every day (e.g. "Waxing day 15, lunar month 12")
• Buddhist holy days (Wan Phra) marked with a small dot
• Thai public holidays and important days: Makha Bucha, Visakha Bucha, Asalha Bucha, Buddhist Lent, Songkran, Loy Krathong and more
• Each feature can be switched on or off on its own

ANY YEAR SYSTEM
• Gregorian (AD), Buddhist Era (BE), Japanese (Reiwa) and Republic of China (Minguo)
• One click to switch between your local era and AD

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• 10 languages: English, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil)
• Month and weekday names follow your language; the week starts on the day your region uses
• Optional week numbers
• Copy today's date in full or short form

LIGHT AND PRIVATE
• Native macOS app with the system glass look
• Works offline. Collects nothing, needs no account and asks for no permissions
• Open at login, if you like

Thai holidays do not include substitute days or one-off holidays announced by the cabinet. Holy days follow the common (Mahanikaya) calendar.
